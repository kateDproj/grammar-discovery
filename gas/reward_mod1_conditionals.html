Conditionals: Type I and Type II

Type Used for Example
I future results, predictions, promises, threats and warnings If we miss the last bus, my dad will come and pick us up.
II unreal situations in the present and situations that are unlikely to happen in the future What would you do if you won the lottery?
Grammar Links

The conditional clause can start with if, even if or unless.

ifIt'll be great if Garry comes.
unlessIt'll be boring unless Garry comes. (= if Garry doesn't come)
even ifCarl won't go to the party even if you beg him.
Grammar Links

The verb in the conditional clauses of the 1st type is in a present tense even though it refers to a future event.

Tense in the if-clauseExample
Present SimpleWe'll have a barbecue if the weather is fine.
Present PerfectI'll cook you something if you haven't eaten.
Present ContinuousIf you're doing your homework when I arrive, I'll be as quiet as a mouse.
Present Perfect ContinuousHe'll be tired when he gets home if he's been working all day.
Grammar Links

In 1st CONDITIONALS the verb in the main clause can be in any of future tenses, structure going to, or Present Simple or Present Continuous in future meaning.

Form in the main clauseExample
Future SimplePaul will do it if you ask him.
Future PerfectThey'll have arrived by one pm unless their plane is late.
Future ContinuousSarah will be waiting for us outside the cinema unless it's raining.
Future Perfect ContinuousIf they don't come in the next five minutes, we'll have been waiting for over an hour.
going toI'm going to scream if you do that again.
Present ContinuousI'm meeting Tanya after school if she doesn't have choir practice.
Present SimpleThe plane arrives at three if it's on schedule.
Grammar Links

If the future result is not certain, we use the modal verbs that express possibility: may, might, can or could.

  • If Hamlet kills Claudius, the guilt could drive him crazy.
  • If you go to Sarah's round dinnertime, you might get something to eat.
  • You may get lost if you go there by the back roads.
  • You can do it if you try.
