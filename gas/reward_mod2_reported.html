REPORTED SPEECH

When we report what someone has said, we can:

  1. 1  repeat their exact words:
    Doctor described Mr G's condition as a 'non-life closer to death'.
  2. 2  report what they said:
    Alison Clarke said that the Government should intervene immediately.
  3. 3  report the idea of what they said using a reporting verb:
    Brutus decides to explain why Julius Caesar had to be killed.
    Don Quixote was still recommended …
    He was threatened with death and torture.
Grammar
Links
  • If you want to report a statement, you use a 'that-clause':
    Alison Clarke said that the Government should intervene immediately.
    Note: 'that' can be omitted.
  • If you want to report a question, you use an 'If-clause' or a 'Wh-clause':
    We asked three people what they thought.
    We asked them if they thought they had made the right decision.
    Note: there is no inversion.
  • If you want to report an order, a request or a piece of advice, you use a 'to-clause':
    She told him to go away.   He asked her to come in.

2. Rewrite the sentences in reported speech.

Напишіть свій варіант і надішліть його — після цього відкриється зразок.

  1. "Take these bags, please!" she asked me.
    → She asked me to take those bags.
  2. "When did you paint this room, yesterday or last week?" my parents inquired.
    Надіслати й показати зразокMy parents inquired when I had painted that room, the day before or the previous week.
  3. "Were you sitting in a pub when the accident happened?" she asked.
    Надіслати й показати зразокShe asked (me) if I had been sitting in a pub when the accident happened.
  4. "We have never tried any drugs," they claim.
    Надіслати й показати зразокThey claim (that) they have never tried any drugs. (Reporting verb in the present → no tense change.)
  5. "They are going to assess our written exams tomorrow," said a student.
    Надіслати й показати зразокA student said (that) they were going to assess their written exams the next day.

3. Turn the sentences into direct speech.

  1. My class mistress wanted to know what I had done during the last lesson.
    → "What have you done during the lesson?" my class mistress asked.
  2. Tom says that they didn't call him so he hasn't done it.
    Надіслати й показати зразок"They didn't call me, so I haven't done it," Tom says.
  3. She told me that her grandpa took a spoon of honey every single morning.
    Надіслати й показати зразок"My grandpa takes a spoon of honey every single morning," she told me.
  4. She reported that they were planning a trip to Paris the following week.
    Надіслати й показати зразок"We are planning a trip to Paris next week," she said.
  5. She wondered if those were his best paintings.
    Надіслати й показати зразок"Are these his best paintings?" she wondered.
