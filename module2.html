Крок 1 · Спостереження

Restaurant Drama

Порівняйте пряму мову з непрямою. Зверніть увагу на виділені фрагменти.

Direct Speech

What actually happened

  • The waiter smiled and said, "What do you recommend?"
  • Harry looked at his wife, "Susan, would you like the crab salad?"
  • The waiter turned to Michelle, "Please, do not smoke here."

Reported Speech

How Emma told the story later

  • The waiter asked what they recommended.
  • Harry asked Susan if she would like the crab salad.
  • The waiter told Michelle not to smoke there.
Крок 2 · Аналіз

Concept Checking Questions

Оберіть одну відповідь на кожне питання.

1. Look at the reported statement: "Harry asked Susan if she would like the crab salad." Which word is used to report a Yes/No question?

2. Look at the reported statement: "The waiter asked what they recommended." Which word connects the clauses when reporting a question with a question word (What, Where, etc.)?

3. In "The waiter told Michelle not to smoke", what form of the verb is used to report an order or request?
