Крок 2 · Аналіз

How do we report different sentences?

Порівняйте кожне речення з тим, що сказали насправді.

1. The waiter said, "The crab salad is always a big hit."
→ The waiter said that the crab salad was always a big hit.This is a statement. Which word introduces the reported statement?

2. Harry asked, "Susan, would you like the crab salad?"
→ Harry asked Susan if she would like the crab salad.The answer to Harry's question is yes or no. Which word introduces the reported question?

3. Harry asked the waiter, "What do you recommend?"
→ Harry asked the waiter what he recommended.This question starts with a question word. Which word introduces the reported question?

4. The waiter said to Michelle, "Please, do not smoke here."
→ The waiter told Michelle not to smoke there.This is a request. Which verb form is used to report it?
