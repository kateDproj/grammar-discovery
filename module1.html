Крок 1 · Спостереження

A Chat About the Future

Прочитайте діалог. Зверніть увагу на виділені фрагменти.

Emma: Are you coming to the party tonight?

David: I will come if you invite me. But I won't finish my homework on time unless I start right away.

Emma: What would you do if you won the lottery?

David: If I had the money, I would buy a huge house. But honestly, if I don't start studying now, I might fail the exam tomorrow!

Крок 2 · Аналіз

Concept Checking Questions

Оберіть одну відповідь на кожне питання.

1. Look at the sentence "I will come if you invite me". Does it refer to a real, possible future or an imaginary situation?

2. Look at "What would you do if you won the lottery?". Does this refer to a real plan or an imaginary situation?

3. In the sentence "I won't finish unless I start right away", what does the word 'unless' mean?

Крок 3 · Формулюємо правило

Complete the rule

Оберіть правильне слово в кожному пропуску.

  1. First Conditionals are used for situations in the future.
  2. The verb in the 'if' clause of the 1st type is usually in a tense.
  3. Second Conditionals are used for situations.
