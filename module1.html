Крок 1 · Спостереження

A Chat About the Future

Прочитайте діалог. Зверніть увагу на виділені фрагменти.

Emma: Are you coming to the party tonight?

David: I will come if you invite me. But I won't finish my homework on time unless I start right away.

Emma: What would you do if you won the lottery?

David: If I had the money, I would buy a huge house. But honestly, if I don't start studying now, I might fail the exam tomorrow!

Крок 2 · Аналіз значення

What do the sentences mean?

Перечитайте речення з діалогу й дайте відповідь на кожне питання.

1. David: "I will come if you invite me."Is it possible that Emma will invite David?

2. David: "If I had the money, I would buy a huge house."Does David have the money now?

3. Emma: "What would you do if you won the lottery?"Does Emma really expect David to win the lottery?

4. David: "I won't finish my homework on time unless I start right away."What does unless mean here?

Крок 4 · Формулюємо правило

Complete the rule

Оберіть правильний варіант у кожному пропуску. Підказки — у ваших відповідях вище.

  1. Sentences like I will come if you invite me are called First Conditionals. They are used for situations in the future.
  2. In First Conditionals, the verb after if is in a tense, even though it refers to the future.
  3. Sentences like If I had the money, I would buy a huge house are called Second Conditionals. They are used for situations.
  4. In Second Conditionals, the verb after if is in a form, but it does not refer to the past. The main clause has + verb.
